    How PAQE is calculated (SOP parts A–C)

    PAQE = Hours × (Outdoor PM2.5 × Infiltration factor) × Breathing rate

    • Outdoor PM2.5: hourly values for your zone's location from Open-Meteo (CAMS model). When Open-Meteo's free daily limit is used up, we use the hourly averages of the official CPCB station for your zone (Patia, Lingraj Mandir, or the nearest one) via OpenAQ. The source is shown under your results. The SOP names the Google Air Quality API, which is a paid service; these free sources give the same hourly history.
    • Infiltration factor (indoor ÷ outdoor): open windows / no filter ≈ 0.7–0.9 (we use 0.8); closed windows / HEPA purifier ≈ 0.1–0.3 (we use 0.2); road and park = 1.0 (direct exposure).
    • Breathing rate: resting / desk work ≈ 0.5 m³/h; light walking ≈ 1.0; commuting 1.2 (as in the SOP sample); heavy exercise / cycling ≈ 2.5–3.0 (we use 2.5).
