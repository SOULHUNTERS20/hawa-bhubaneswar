    How it's calculated

    E = Σ ( Ci × Ti × IRi )

    • Ci: PM2.5 in each place = outdoor PM2.5 × infiltration factor (× (1 − mask filtration) on the commute).
    • Ti: hours spent there. IRi: breathing rate in m³/hour (higher when exercising).
    • pAQI converts your total dose back into the AQI of the air a resting adult (0.5 m³/h) would have to breathe for 24 hours to get the same dose.
    • Factors are typical values from exposure-science literature, rounded. This is an educational estimate, not a measurement.
